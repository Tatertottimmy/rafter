      In the embedded preview, schematics download as a .zip; on the hosted site they download directly.

      .litematic — for the Litematica mod (Fabric). Drop the file into .minecraft/schematics, then load it from the Litematica menu and place it as a schematic to build against, or paste it with Easy Place / a creative-mode paste.

      .schem — Sponge v2 schematic for WorldEdit / FAWE. Put it in plugins/WorldEdit/schematics (or config/worldedit/schematics) and run //schem load <name> then //paste.

      The schematic origin is the lowest north-west corner of the design; roof stairs carry their facing and corner shapes so they paste exactly as drawn.
